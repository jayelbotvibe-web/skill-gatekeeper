The Library Analogy

How the Skill Gatekeeper works — from arrival to answer

SCENARIO A — Happy Path

"Research volcanoes" → geology mode

Gatekeeper scans keywords: "research" + "volcanoes" → research mode, score 4. Moves 91 books to back room. Librarian recites 19 books in 12 seconds instead of 110 books in 41 seconds. Pure savings on every turn.

SCENARIO B — Unsure Visitor

"Uhh, just looking around" → all mode

Gatekeeper scans: no clear signal. Score below 1 → all mode. Does nothing. All 110 books stay on shelves. Librarian recites everything. Safe default — better to waste tokens than hide a book the visitor might need.

SCENARIO C — Sudden Swerve

Mid-research, needs calendar → mode switch

Gatekeeper notices confusion. Taps your shoulder: "Switch to admin section?" You say yes. 30 sec to rearrange shelves. Calendar checked. 30 sec to put geology back. Four turns of overhead for one calendar lookup. The price of specialization.

SCENARIO D — Two at Once

Two visitors, two gatekeepers → file lock

Visitor A wants volcanoes. Visitor B wants French cooking. Both gatekeepers reach for shelves. Lock on the storage room door prevents chaos — only one can move books at a time. But Visitor B's librarian may recite a stale catalog. Re-scan needed.

SCENARIO E — Trip and Fall

Gatekeeper crashes mid-sprint → in_progress flag

Halfway through moving books. Falls. Books scattered. Gets up, checks clipboard: in_progress: true. Refuses to continue. Points to big red reset button. Everything back on shelves. Try again. No books lost.

SCENARIO F — Wrong Guess

"Volcanic security vulnerabilities" → misclassified

Sees "security" → guesses cybersecurity. Moves geology to back room. Librarian has no volcano books. Failure is immediate and visible. Reset button. All 110 books return. Fail-safe by design.
